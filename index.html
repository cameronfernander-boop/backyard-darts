How to play
301 – 1001 (X01)

Everyone starts on the same score (301, 501, 701 or 1001) and subtracts what they score with each turn of three darts. You must finish on exactly zero, and the last dart has to land in a double or the bullseye. If you go below zero, or leave yourself on 1 with double-out on, it's a bust and your score goes back to what it was before that turn. In the app: add up your three darts, type the total, and tap Enter.

Cricket

The targets are 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20 and the bull. Three marks closes a number: a single is one mark, a double is two, a triple is three (bull: outer is one, inner is two). Once you've closed a number, more hits on it score points, until all your opponents have closed it too. To win, close all seven targets while having at least as many points as everyone else. In the app: pick Single, Double or Triple, then tap the number you hit for each dart.

Around the World

Race to hit every number in order: 1, then 2, then 3, all the way to 20, and finish on the bullseye (outer or inner). You only move forward when you hit your current number. The first player to hit the bullseye wins. Optional rule: a double moves you forward 2 numbers and a triple moves you forward 3. In the app: tap Hit or Miss for each dart.

Shanghai

There are 7 rounds, and the target number is the round number (1 in round 1, 2 in round 2, and so on). You throw 3 darts at the target. A single scores the number, a double scores it twice and a triple three times. Hit a single, a double and a triple of the target in the same turn and that's a Shanghai: you win on the spot. If nobody does it, the highest score after round 7 wins. In the app: tap Single, Double, Triple or Miss for each dart.

Bob's 27

A practice game for doubles. You start on 27 and work through double 1, double 2 and so on up to double 20, then the bull, which makes 21 rounds. Each round you throw 3 darts at that double. Every hit adds the double's value to your score (double 5 is worth 10, the bull is worth 50). If all three darts miss, you lose that value. If your score drops below zero you're out. The highest score at the end wins. In the app: tap how many of your 3 darts hit.

Halve-It

There are 9 rounds, each with its own target: 15, 16, 17, any double, 18, 19, 20, any triple and the bull. You throw 3 darts at the target. Whatever you hit on the target is added to your total. If all three darts miss, your total is cut in half. The highest total at the end wins. In the app: type the points you scored with your 3 darts and tap Enter, or tap "Missed all 3 darts" to halve your total.

Killer

Each player is given their own double, picked at random, and starts with 3 lives. Hit your own double three times to become a killer. Once you're a killer, every hit on another player's double takes away one of their lives. Lose all three and you're out. The last player left wins. You need at least 2 players. In the app: tap the double you hit for each dart, or Miss.
